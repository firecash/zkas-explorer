How to read this

  1. Each crate is a block. A new one every second, rolling left. The lines link it to the blocks it confirms.
  2. Two crates at once? Both are kept — this network throws nothing away.
  3. Capsules are freshly minted coins, sealed into a parcel the moment they leave the block.
  4. Envelopes are payments. You can see one happened and how many parcels moved. Not who, not how much.
  5. The pool holds every coin, sealed. Bubbles mean someone spent a parcel — which one, nobody can tell.
This page is blind on purpose: there are no addresses, amounts or balances to show — the network itself can't see them. Open the explorer →
