What am I looking at?

  1. Crates are blocks. One arrives every second and rolls left; the threads point at the blocks it builds on.
  2. Stacked crates are DAG siblings — found in the same second, all kept. An orange outline means a reorg dropped it.
  3. Capsules are sealed notes. Each block mints its reward straight into sealed form.
  4. Envelopes are people's transfers. You can see one happened and how many parts it has. Nothing else.
  5. The pool is the shielded pool. Notes sink in and stay. Bubbles rising = a note somewhere was spent.
